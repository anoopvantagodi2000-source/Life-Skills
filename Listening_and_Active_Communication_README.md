# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Give the speaker your full attention.
2. Avoid checking your phone or doing other tasks.
3. Let the person finish before you respond.
4. Maintain suitable eye contact and open body language.
5. Ask questions when something is not clear.
6. Show interest through small responses such as “I understand” or “Go on.”
7. Repeat the main points in your own words to check your understanding.
8. Avoid judging or preparing your reply while the other person is speaking.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- Let the other person explain their thoughts without interrupting.
- Pay attention to the important facts and details.
- Take notes when the conversation is important.
- Repeat or summarise what you understood in your own words.
- Keep an open mind, even when you disagree.
- Show genuine interest and try to understand the speaker's point of view.
- Ask the speaker to confirm whether you understood them correctly.

## 3. Reflection

### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- Sometimes my mind wanders while someone is speaking.
- I get distracted by my own thoughts.
- At times, I start thinking about what to reply instead of listening fully.
- I may hesitate to ask questions when something is unclear.

### Question 4: What can I do to improve my listening?

- Keep my phone and other distractions away.
- Focus on the speaker instead of planning my reply.
- Avoid interrupting.
- Ask questions whenever I need clarification.
- Summarise important points to confirm my understanding.
- Take notes during important discussions.

## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

I sometimes become passive when I am unsure of myself or when I do not want to disagree with someone. I stay quiet even when I have a different opinion.

### Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

When I feel frustrated or when things do not go as expected, I may respond impatiently or speak in a harsh tone. I need to pause before reacting.

### Question 7: When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes, instead of explaining what is bothering me, I may become quiet, use sarcasm, or expect the other person to understand the problem on their own.

### Question 8: How can I make my communication assertive?

- Express my thoughts clearly and respectfully.
- Say what I need instead of expecting others to guess.
- Use “I feel” or “I think” statements instead of blaming others.
- Listen to the other person's opinion, even when I disagree.
- Say no politely when necessary.
- Discuss problems early instead of keeping them inside.
- Stay calm and focus on finding a solution.
